❓ FAQ

Frequent questions

How long does the audit take?

24-48 hours after we receive your email with payment proof. Manual review — quality over speed.

What if my token fails the audit?

You receive the full report with issues found. €AUDT is not refunded — the scan was performed. You can fix issues and resubmit.

Can I revoke my audit?

Yes, but the record stays on-chain. Audit status is public — anyone can verify.

Do I need to send €AUDT first, or email first?

Send €AUDT first, then email with the transaction hash. We verify payment, then start the audit.

What checks are performed?

Mint Authority, Freeze Authority, Holder Distribution, LP Lock, Honeypot detection, Transfer Fee analysis. Deep level adds Transfer Hook audit and contract logic review.

Can I submit a token that's not on Solana?

No. €AUDT only audits Solana tokens (SPL and Token-2022).
